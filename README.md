# Kavach 

**Your family's protection, all in one place.**

Kavach is an AI-powered insurance management platform designed to simplify the insurance lifecycle by bringing policy management, renewals, claims, and family protection into one unified digital experience.

### Key Features

- **Protection Dashboard:** Get an overview of active policies, upcoming renewals, claims, and pending requests.
- **Policy Wallet:** Manage and view insurance policies in one place.
- **Digital Policy Surrender:** Simplify policy cancellation with upfront surrender value, OTP verification, and e-sign.
- **Smart Renewal Centre:** Receive renewal reminders and manage policy renewals.
- **ClaimEasy:** Simplify claim filing with AI-assisted form filling and document verification.
- **Document Vault:** Securely organize insurance-related documents.
- **Indus AI:** AI-powered insurance assistance and coverage gap detection.
- **Family Protection Hub:** Manage and monitor insurance coverage for family members.
- **Emergency Assistance:** Access essential emergency support services.

### Project Objective

To make insurance more accessible, transparent, and convenient by reducing paperwork, eliminating unnecessary branch visits, and simplifying complex insurance processes through a digital-first approach.

### Vision

Making insurance easier to understand, manage, claim, and surrender — all from one place.
